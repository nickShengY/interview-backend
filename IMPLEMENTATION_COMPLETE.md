## 📚 LEARNING SCIENCE BEHIND TEXTBOOK MODULE

### Why Spaced Repetition Works:
- **Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve:** We forget 50% within 1 hour, 70% within 24 hours
- **Spacing Effect:** Information reviewed at intervals is retained 200-300% better
- **SuperMemo Algorithm:** Optimizes review schedule based on individual performance

### Why Active Recall Works:
- **Retrieval Practice:** Testing yourself is 2-3x more effective than re-reading
- **Generation Effect:** Producing answers strengthens memory pathways
- **Metacognition:** Identifies what you don't know

### Why Feynman Technique Works:
- **Elaborative Encoding:** Explaining concepts creates richer memory traces
- **Gap Identification:** Reveals areas of confusion immediately
- **Conceptual Understanding:** Moves beyond memorization to true comprehension

### Research References:
- Karpicke & Roediger (2008): "The Critical Importance of Retrieval for Learning"
- Cepeda et al. (2006): "Distributed Practice in Verbal Recall Tasks"
- Dunlosky et al. (2013): "Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques"
